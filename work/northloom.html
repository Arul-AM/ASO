E-COMMERCE

A furniture storefront that stopped losing customers at checkout.

41% faster checkout — a case study on how we approached Northloom Studio's project.

Client
Northloom Studio
Industry
Furniture & Home Goods
Timeline
3 weeks
Services
Web Design, Development, E-Commerce

The Challenge

Northloom's old store ran on a bloated page-builder theme. Product pages loaded slowly, checkout took nine steps, and mobile customers were dropping off before completing an order.

Our Approach

We rebuilt the storefront from scratch on a lean stack — stripped the checkout down to three steps, compressed and lazy-loaded product imagery, and rebuilt navigation around how customers actually browse furniture: by room, not by SKU.

The Result

Checkout completion time dropped 41%, and mobile abandonment fell noticeably in the first month post-launch. The new site now loads in under two seconds on average connections.

"They shipped in three weeks what our last agency couldn't finish in three months."

Riya Nambiar, Founder, Northloom Studio
